# User Guide (planned behaviour)

Nothing is implemented yet. Planned modes:

- **Record & Reverse:** record for a chosen time, wait, then hear the take backwards. Five seconds of recording plus two seconds of wait starts playback at seven seconds.
- **Live Reverse:** reverse completed chunks continuously, with adjustable chunk size and extra delay.
- **Reverse File:** load WAV, AIFF or FLAC, choose a region, audition and export.

Extras planned: forward/backward comparison, hold-to-record, replay, loops, ping-pong, tape-speed changes, live freeze,
voice triggering, countdown, reversible silence trimming, presets and WAV export.
